> Irrigation should be considered for Area 1 because soil moisture (51.7 %) is below the optimal minimum of 60 % (target 65 %), soil moisture is falling at 10.303 %/h and the current temperature is high (30 °C, above the optimal maximum of 25 °C). Verify water availability before activating the irrigation system.
